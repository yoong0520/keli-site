K⁠-⁠University AI EdTech and Learning Initiative

A higher⁠-⁠education AI ecosystem,
built together by universities.

KELI is a national⁠-⁠level shared platform.
Hallym University develops and classroom⁠-⁠tests AI education services (HAI Ecosystems) and provides them to universities and institutions in Korea and abroad.

Contact us한국어

What KELI offers.

HAI Ecosystems

19 AI education services developed and classroom⁠-⁠tested by Hallym University, from course design to assessment and analytics.

KELI Marketplace

An online portal to browse services and request adoption.

Pilot and training support

One⁠-⁠semester pilot operation, faculty training and an effectiveness report.

How to join.

Every participation starts with the KELI Secretariat.
First step: one course, one service, one semester.

1Inquiry

Email the secretariat with your institution, service of interest and target course.

2Consultation & demo

Free demonstration and guidance.

3Pilot

One⁠-⁠semester trial in a real course.

4Adoption

Subscription or license agreed with the secretariat.
